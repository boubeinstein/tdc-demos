How to use the board

Edit a card
Double-tap (or double-click) the card text, type, then tap elsewhere or press Enter.
Move a card
Drag it anywhere on the wall. Build the chart re-arranges everything into the hierarchy; Brainstorm scatters the cards as at the start of a session.
Add a card
+ Add card, choose the card type, then link it to its parent (below) and use Build the chart to place it.
Link a card to its parent
Tap Link, tap the card, then tap its parent. The hierarchy is enforced: subtask → task → function → job title.
Unlink or move a card to another parent
In Link mode, tap the card twice to unlink it — or tap the card and then a different parent to re-link it directly.
Delete a card
Hover or tap the card and use the × in its corner. Deletion is bottom-up: a card with linked cards beneath it cannot be deleted until they are deleted or re-linked.
Undo a deletion
Press Ctrl+Z (⌘+Z on Mac), or tap Undo on the message that appears after deleting. Deletions can be undone one by one, most recent first.
Present
Present clears the wall and reveals one level per tap or Space: job title, functions, tasks, subtasks.
Numbering
Numbers follow the hierarchy automatically: functions are numbered in order (1.0, 2.0…), a task takes its number when linked to a function (2.1, 2.2…), a subtask when linked to a task (2.1.1…). Deleting or moving a card renumbers the rest.
Save and open boards
Boards saves the current board under a name on this device, opens a saved one, starts a new board, or exports/imports a board as a file to share between devices. Your work is also saved automatically as you go.
Titles
The job title card, the group name and the batch label in the top bar are all editable — tap the dotted text. The board remembers them on this device.
